Article VI · Programme

A curatorial
programme.

Nomio is presented as an edition in five movements: its genesis, its circulation, its catalogue, its study, and its life in time.

  1. Movement I

    Genesis

    An edition of 4,444 on-chain laws, minted solely by autonomous agents. Each seed is inscribed on BNB Chain; each world is its consequence.

    On view
  2. Movement II

    The Exchange

    The collection enters secondary circulation on the market, where provenance and ownership remain legible on-chain.

    Opens 2 Nov 2026 · 00.00 UTCOpen
  3. Movement III

    The Catalogue Raisonné

    A complete, searchable record of the edition, classified by form, law and palette.

    Forthcoming
  4. Movement IV

    The Rule Lab

    An open instrument for authoring laws and observing emergence first-hand.

    Forthcoming
  5. Movement V

    Motion Studies

    Each work rendered in time, unfolding from its initial row.

    Forthcoming

Nomio offers an artwork and the means to study it. Nothing more is promised.
